OUR BELIEFS

our beliefs

These principles guide our lives, our ministry, and all that we do, rooted in the Gospel of Christ.

01

Scriptures Inspired

The Bible is God's infallible Word, guiding faith and conduct.

02

The One True God

God reveals Himself as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

03

Man's Fall and Redemption

Man fell through sin, and redemption is only through Jesus Christ.

04

Salvation

Salvation is by God's grace, through faith in Jesus Christ.

05

Promise of the Holy Spirit

Believers should seek the Holy Spirit for power in life and ministry.

06

Sanctification

God calls believers to holiness, empowered by the Holy Spirit.

07

The Church

The church is the Body of Christ, fulfilling His commission.

08

Ministry

God provides ministers for evangelism and building up the church.

09

Marriage

Marriage is a covenant between a man and a woman.
