what we
believe

These principles guide our lives, our ministry, and all that we do, rooted in the Gospel of Christ. Our faith is grounded in Scripture and centered on Jesus.

01

Scriptures Inspired

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and our trustworthy guide for faith and life.

02

The One True God

We believe in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

03

Man’s Fall and Redemption

Humanity has sinned and needs redemption, made possible through Jesus Christ.

04

Salvation

We are saved by God's grace through faith in Jesus Christ and His finished work.

05

Promise of the Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit empowers believers to witness boldly and serve the mission of Jesus.

06

Sanctification

God calls His people to grow in holiness and become more like Jesus.

07

The Church

The Church is Christ's people, gathered to worship, make disciples, and carry the Gospel.

08

Ministry

Every believer can serve others and take the good news of Jesus into the world.

09

Marriage

We honor marriage as a covenant before God and seek to reflect His faithfulness in our homes.

10

Divine Healing

We pray in faith for God's healing and care, trusting His wisdom and compassion.

11

The Blessed Hope

We look forward to the return of Jesus and the hope promised to His people.

12

Millennial Reign

We believe in Christ's promised reign and the fulfillment of God's purposes.

13

New Heavens and Earth

We look toward God's promised renewal of creation and everlasting life with Him.
