Why We Exist

The World Needs
Jesus.

Jesus To The World Ministry is committed to bringing the message of salvation beyond the four walls of a church. Through evangelism, preaching, digital media, and personal outreach, we seek to point people toward repentance, faith, and a transformed life in Jesus Christ.

Our heart is for those who feel forgotten, those searching for purpose, and those who have never truly heard the Gospel. We believe the message of Jesus is not meant to be hidden. It is meant to be proclaimed.

“For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost.”

LUKE 19:10 · KJV
